Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

NewLeaf doesn’t collect your data.

There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything NewLeaf knows stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

NewLeaf is made by Sean Jones, an independent developer. If you have a question about this policy, email seanjonesbusiness@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, NewLeaf keeps a few things on your phone. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

  • Your block list. You choose apps and websites with Apple’s Screen Time picker. Apple gives NewLeaf only anonymous tokens for your choices, so NewLeaf can’t see which apps or sites you picked, and never sees how you use them.
  • Your streak and garden. The dates your streak started and ended, and the trees on your shelves.
  • Your sponsor pairing. The secret behind your sponsor’s codes is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only. It is never synced to iCloud and never shown in the app.
  • Your settings, such as light or dark mode and whether gambling websites are blocked.

Screen Time and Family Controls

NewLeaf uses Apple’s Screen Time features only to shield the apps and websites on your block list and to bring the shield back after an unlock. This data is handled by iOS on your device. NewLeaf doesn’t read your Screen Time reports or app usage.

Your sponsor

When you pair, your sponsor scans a QR code with an authenticator app on their own phone. The code goes straight from your screen to their phone; nothing passes through us. Their authenticator app has its own privacy policy.

When you choose to share something

  • Message my sponsor opens the iPhone share sheet. Your message is sent by the app you pick, such as Messages or WhatsApp. NewLeaf doesn’t see or keep it.
  • Helplines open your phone app or web browser. NewLeaf doesn’t know whether you call.
  • Send feedback opens your email app. If you send us an email, we receive your email address, your message, and the app and iOS versions included at the bottom. We use it only to reply and fix problems, and don’t share it.

Notifications

NewLeaf shows a notification on your phone when you tap “Request unlock” on a blocked app. It is created on your device. We don’t send push notifications.

Deleting your data

Deleting NewLeaf removes its shields and its data from your phone. iOS may keep the Keychain items (your pairing and streak) on the phone after the app is deleted, so reinstalling NewLeaf on the same phone can restore them. Erasing your iPhone removes them completely. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Age

NewLeaf is meant for adults. It isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. If NewLeaf ever starts collecting data, the policy will say so before that happens.

My sponsor can’t give me a code

That’s by design. Only your sponsor’s current code can unlock NewLeaf, change your block list or turn the shield off. There is no backup code and no way for us to unlock it. Ask your sponsor to keep their authenticator app, and stay in touch with them.

My sponsor got a new phone

Most authenticator apps can move codes to a new phone. If your sponsor can still see their code, you can also pair a new sponsor from the Sponsor tab using their current code.

Why do I have to wait before unlocking?

Urges usually rise and pass within minutes. The short pause gives you time to check whether you still want to go ahead, and to message your sponsor or call a helpline first.

What NewLeaf can’t do

  • Someone determined can still delete the app or reset the phone. Your sponsor can set a Screen Time passcode to make deleting apps harder.
  • It blocks the apps you choose and 50 major gambling websites. Other sites and apps aren’t covered unless you add them.
  • Codes are checked on your phone, so a very technical user could get around them. NewLeaf is a support, not a guarantee.
